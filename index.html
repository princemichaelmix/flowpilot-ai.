How FlowPilot AI helps you follow up

A simple daily routine for a small business.

1. Save every enquiry
When someone asks about a product or service, add their name, what they want, and where they contacted you.
2. Set a follow-up date
Choose when you want to contact them again. Check the tracker each morning.
3. Prepare a personal message
Select the lead, choose the goal, generate a draft, and edit it to sound like you.
4. Contact them in your usual app
Copy the message into WhatsApp, email, or another channel. This version does not send messages on your behalf.
5. Update the result
Mark whether they need another follow-up, became a customer, or are no longer interested.

Next production steps: account sign-in, shared cloud storage, real scheduled reminders, and approved messaging integrations. These are required before this can be a multi-user paid service.
